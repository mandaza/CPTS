- Proof of Concept (PoC) or Proof of Principle refers to a project management term that serves as proof that a project is feasible in principle. 
- It serves as a decision-making basis for the further course of action. At the same time, it enables risks to be identified and minimised. 
- This project step is often integrated into the development process for new application software(prototyping) or IT security solutions. 
- As of use we uses it to prove the security problem exist so that the developers or administrator can validate it, reproduce it, see the impact, and test their remediation efforts. 
- One of the most common examples used to prove software vulnerabilities is executing the calculator (calc.ext on Windows) on the target system.
