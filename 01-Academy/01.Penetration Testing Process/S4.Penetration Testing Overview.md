A penetration Test (PenTest) is an organised, targeted, and authorised attack attempt to test IT infrastructure and its defenders to determine their susceptibility to IT security vulnerabilities. A pentest uses *==🟢methods and techniques that real attackers use==*. As penetration testers, we apply various techniques and analyses to gauge the impact that a particular vulnerability or chain of vulnerabilities may have on the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of an organization's IT systems and data.
