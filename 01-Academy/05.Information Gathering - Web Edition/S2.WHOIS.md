## WHOIS

Query/response protocol for databases of registered internet resources — **domains**, IP blocks, autonomous systems. The internet's "phonebook."

```bash
whois inlanefreight.com
```

## What a record contains
- **Domain Name**
- **Registrar** (where it was registered — GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.)
- **Registrant / Administrative / Technical contact** — names, emails, phones
- **Creation & Expiration dates**
- **Name Servers** (resolve the domain → IP)

## Why it matters for recon
- **Key personnel** — contact names/emails/phones → **phishing / social engineering** targets.
- **Network infrastructure** — name servers + IPs hint at infra, entry points, misconfigs.
- **Historical data** — services like **WhoisFreaks** show ownership/contact/tech changes over time.

## Notes to self
- Passive-ish, **very low detection** — safe early step.
- Grab: contacts (people to target), name servers (→ feed DNS enumeration), registrar, dates.
- Privacy protection often masks registrant details on public domains; internal/older domains leak more.
